## Subprocessor Notification & Objection Policy

1. **Advance Notice:** CampusPulse provides at least thirty (30) days' advance notice to institutional customers before authorizing any new subprocessor.
2. **Notification Channel:** Notices are published to this registry and emailed to designated institutional administrative contacts.
3. **Right to Object:** Customers may submit reasonable objections to new subprocessors on data protection grounds within fifteen (15) days of notice. If an objection cannot be mutually resolved, the customer may terminate the affected services without penalty.
