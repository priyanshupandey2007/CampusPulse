## 1. Scope and Processing Roles

- **Controller Status:** Customer is the controller of all student records, academic telemetry, and faculty intervention records ("Customer Personal Data").
- **Processor Status:** CampusPulse processes Customer Personal Data solely on behalf of Customer and in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and this DPA.

---

## 2. Details of Processing (GDPR Article 28(3))

- **Subject Matter:** The provision of the CampusPulse student success and placement readiness platform.
- **Duration:** The term of the subscription agreement plus any period required for data return or deletion.
- **Nature and Purpose:** Ingesting academic, attendance, and assessment metrics to compute deterministic success scores, triage high-risk students, and track faculty mentoring interventions.
- **Data Categories:** Student roll numbers, names, emails, academic indexes (CGPA, backlogs), attendance rates, coding scores, and advisor intervention notes. Excludes all sensitive demographic traits (caste, religion, biometric, health).
- **Categories of Data Subjects:** Admitted university students and authorized academic personnel.

---

## 3. Processor Obligations

1. **Documented Instructions:** Processor shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller.
2. **Confidentiality:** Processor ensures that all personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are committed to confidentiality.
3. **Security Measures:** Processor implements technical and organizational measures (TOMs), including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), rate limiting, and access control.
4. **Subprocessors:** Processor shall not engage subprocessors without prior written authorization in accordance with the Subprocessor Registry.
5. **Assistance with Rights:** Processor provides technical capabilities for Controller to fulfill data subject requests (access, export, rectification, erasure).
6. **Data Breach Notification:** Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay (and in any event within 48 hours) upon becoming aware of a confirmed personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.
7. **Deletion and Return:** Upon termination of services, Processor shall, at Controller's choice, delete or return all Customer Personal Data within thirty (30) days.
