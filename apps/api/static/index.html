A clear, simple explanation of how student scores are calculated from 6 key areas, balanced fairly, and protected by clear safety rules without hidden formulas.

1. How the Overall Student Success Score Is Calculated

The overall Student Success Score (from 0 to 100) is calculated by combining 6 balanced performance areas:

S = 0.25 / I_academic + 0.20 / I_attendance + 0.20 / I_placement + 0.15 / I_lms + 0.10 / I_engagement + 0.10 / I_skills
Constraint: \(\sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i = 1.0000000000\) (Cryptographically verified at boot: c4520cb10d79)

The 6 Performance Areas & Formulas

Dimension Weight Mathematical Formula Variables & Raw Data Sources Normalization & Bounds
Academic Index (\(I_{\text{acad}}\)) 25% clamp(CGPA × 10 - 3 × min(backlogs, 5), 0, 100) CGPA (0-10), Active Backlog Count from SIS / ERP Clamped to \([0, 100]\) with 3-point backlog deduction
Attendance Index (\(I_{\text{att}}\)) 20% clamp(Mean_Att - 5 × count(sub_att < 60%), 0, 100) Overall % + Subject-wise attendance from RFID / Biometric Clamped to \([0, 100]\) with 5-point penalty per critical subject
Placement Readiness (\(I_{\text{place}}\)) 20% 0.35 × Aptitude + 0.40 × Coding + 0.25 × MockInterview Aptitude assessments, DSA code editor runs, Mock interview scores Weighted sum of verified technical competencies
LMS Activity (\(I_{\text{lms}}\)) 15% 0.50 × AssignmentCompletion% + 0.50 × ScaledLogins Moodle/Canvas submissions, Active daily learning sessions Normalized log of weekly interactions & submission rates
Engagement (\(I_{\text{eng}}\)) 10% min(100, (Events + Clubs + 2×Hackathons + 2×Certs)/20 × 100) Hackathon participation, Tech clubs, Verified certificates Linear scaling up to institutional cap of 20 weighted credits
Skills Inventory (\(I_{\text{skills}}\)) 10% 0.60 × TechSkillScore + 0.40 × SoftSkillScore Technical stack assessments + Communication evaluations Holistic industry competency synthesis

2. Clear Safety Guardrails (Transparent & Explainable)

Sometimes an overall average score can hide a critical problem — like a student who is great at coding but might miss exams because their attendance is too low. These safety rules automatically highlight students who need quick help so no one falls through the cracks.

Attendance Safety Rule
Attendance < 60.0%
Marks student for Priority Support and alerts their advisor right away so they don't get held back from exams.
Backlog Safety Rule
Active Backlogs ≥ 2
Marks student for Priority Support so teachers can help them clear pending subjects quickly.
CGPA / Grades Safety Rule
CGPA < 5.0
Alerts advisors immediately to arrange extra tutoring and concept review sessions.

3. How Students Are Prioritized for Advisor Support

Teachers and advisors have busy schedules. CampusPulse organizes the student list so advisors instantly see students who need the most urgent support:

P = (100 - S) + 50 / \mathbb{I}_{\text{HardFloor}} + 15 / \mathbb{I}_{\text{Momentum}=\text{DOWN}} + 10 / \mathbb{I}_{\text{OverdueIntervention}}
Benefit: Helps advisors reach students who need urgent help first, ensuring no one is overlooked.
